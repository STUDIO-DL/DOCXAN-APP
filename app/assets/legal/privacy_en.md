# Docxan privacy policy

**Draft for publication.** Replace the URL in Play Console once it is hosted.

Docxan digitizes documents on the device. OCR, compression, and everyday storage are local.

If you turn on backup, files are encrypted on the phone (AES-256-GCM) before they are uploaded to Cloudflare R2. Docxan cannot read the contents of those backups. If you forget your PIN and have no other recovery method, the backup cannot be restored.

The account (email) is managed with Supabase Auth. We do not sell data. You can request deletion of the account and of the associated blobs.

Contact: privacy@docxan.app
